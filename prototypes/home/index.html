HOME→COLLECTION

Four sketches for how the homepage can surface the full collection — without breaking what already works. Each one is a live prototype against index.html; real marquee, real images, real links into explore/grid.html.

Constraints guiding these sketches. Homepage should still feel like a title card, not a menu. The entry point should be discoverable without being loud — the collection is "images I like, ready to save or share", so the affordance should read as an aside, not a call-to-action. The band-scrolling takes priority on hover + drag, so any new element has to sit outside those zones or in a dedicated strip.
A
CORNER PILL

A single glass pill fades in at the top-right, ~1s after the page settles. Mirrors the existing nav-pills pattern used on explore/grid.html — so the same chrome is quietly present from the first screen. The title, the bands, and the dead zone around them are untouched.

Interaction
Hover → inverts black. Click → /explore/grid.html.
Strengths
Most discoverable. Consistent with the rest of the site. Zero impact on the hero.
Trade-off
Feels like "a website" — less like a one-off story. Lives in the same corner as future sign-in / menu.

TITLE CAPTION

A small line in the same Bebas Neue, centered beneath "LOVE AND DEATH": — AND EVERYTHING ELSE →. It reads as a continuation of the title copy rather than navigation chrome, so the collection feels like part of the same sentence as the hero. Arrow drifts softly to signal action.

Interaction

Strengths
Most "personal project" in tone. Type hierarchy is clean. Keeps the center of gravity where it already is.
Trade-off
Tied to fixed center — reduces breathing room around the title. A new visitor might read it as just more tagline.

C
DOORWAY · SLIDE-UP

Your gut feeling, made to work — with your own upgrade. A 56px dark strip in the collection's own #0d0d0d sits below the bottom band. Clicking THE COLLECTION ↓ lifts the whole homepage straight up, revealing the dark archive beneath. The ↓ arrow becomes literal: you push the homepage up, you descend into what was underneath. The doorway doubles as a colophon — left side carries the site's identity (A PERSONAL ARCHIVE · ONGOING), right side is the entry.

Interaction
Hover → strip saturates to black. Click → homepage slides up ~0.85s, then /explore/grid.html loads.
Strengths
Strong spatial metaphor. Doorway carries identity + entry. The transition is the entry — no jarring cut between pages.
Trade-off
Bottom band shifted up 56px. Slide-up adds ~0.8s before the grid loads — a taste decision.

D
SCROLL CURTAIN

A hint pill sits at the very bottom: SCROLL DOWN FOR THE COLLECTION ↓. Scrolling (or swiping up on touch) pulls a dark curtain up from the bottom in sync with the gesture — a live preview of the transition. At 100% progress, the curtain seals and navigates. Clicking the pill plays the same animation to completion for discoverability.

Interaction
Scroll wheel / touch-swipe up progressively reveals the curtain. Click the pill to auto-play.
Strengths
Cinematic. Rewards curiosity. The transition is the entry — feels like part of the piece.
Trade-off
Heaviest to build and tune. Scroll-hijacking is a strong choice — needs to feel earned, not imposed.
